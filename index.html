01 ABOUT US

People first.
Purpose always.

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation founded in 2020 with a vision to create meaningful social change.

Through community-driven initiatives, the foundation works across areas including education, women's empowerment, environmental conservation, animal welfare and skill development.
